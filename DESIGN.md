# Design System: SIMUTU

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Frosted Glass over Teal-Lavender Mist"**

SIMUTU is a daily working tool for quality-assurance staff, leadership, auditors, lecturers and students. Its world is a pale mist that drifts from mint-teal at the top left to lavender at the right and bottom corners, fixed behind the content while it scrolls. Every surface sitting on that mist is a translucent, blurred glass panel with a bright one-pixel white edge and a soft, offset teal-tinted shadow. Deep teal is the one voice of action: the primary button, the active navigation pill, and the dashboard/login banner. Violet, pink, orange, sky and teal appear only as small gradient icon chips and data encodings, never as surface fills.

The build rejects the flat admin dashboard (grey page, flat white cards, dark sidebar) in favour of a light, calm workspace. Density stays high: KPI tiles, tables, meters and charts sit side by side at 14px body text. Ink is dark teal, never black. The world is light-only; there is no dark mode.

Accessibility shaped the palette. Every text tone was darkened until it clears WCAG AA against the thinnest glass (primary text, muted text, danger, violet and orange inks all have their own deeper "ink" token), and the banner teal was taken darker than first planned so white text holds.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Fixed multi-radial mist background; glass surfaces float on it with blur, a white edge and a soft teal shadow.
- Gradient everywhere: primary buttons, active pill, banners, meters, chips and soft status badges are all gentle two-stop gradients.
- One deep-teal action colour; accent hues are confined to icon chips, badges and charts.
- Dark teal ink with separate AA-safe ink tokens for each tinted tone.
- Generous roundness (14px base, 22px cards, 24px banners, pill badges).
- Plus Jakarta Sans throughout, extrabold for numbers and headings, tabular figures for data.

## Colors

A cool teal-and-lavender atmosphere carrying one deep-teal action colour, with warm orange, violet and coral kept to small signals.

### Primary
- **Deep Action Teal** (action-teal): text, icons, links, focus rings, caret and form accent. At ≥4.5:1 on the thinnest glass it is the only teal allowed for text.
- **Action Teal Gradient** (action-teal-grad-start → action-teal-grad-end, 135deg): fills the primary button, the active sidebar pill and the "good" meter. White text on it holds at ≥4.65:1. Buttons hover by brightening 10%; the hover gradient token (see Gradients) darkens the stops instead.
- **Banner Teal** (hero-teal-start → hero-mid → hero-teal-end, 120deg, with a faint aqua highlight and a hero-shade lower corner; hero-mid is also the solid fallback): the dashboard greeting banner, the login brand panel, the student portal banner, the lecturer analytics panel and tooltips. It is darker than the direction contract's first sketch (#0f7f86 → #16a39a) on purpose, so white body text and the warm accent both pass. Text and focus rings on it use **On-Banner White** (on-hero); a faint dotted texture uses **Banner Sparkle** (hero-sparkle), and the blurred dark blobs use hero-shade. **Banner Apricot** (hero-accent) is the only accent colour allowed on it; up/down deltas on it use on-dark-up and on-dark-down.

### Secondary
- **Warm Orange** (warm-orange, orange gradient): the warm accent from the reference, used for the active-period pill, notification counters, "gold" icon chips and warning meters. The token is still named `gold` in code for compatibility; it means warm orange. Text in this family always uses **Burnt Orange Ink** (warm-orange-ink) on **Apricot Wash** (warm-orange-soft); numbers printed directly on the orange counter gradient use **Umber** (on-orange).

### Tertiary
- **Soft Violet** (violet, violet gradient): the info tone, the "in progress/review" badge, info icon chips and text selection tint. Violet text uses **Deep Violet Ink** (violet-ink) on violet-soft.
- **Coral Rose** (coral-danger, danger gradient): destructive buttons, critical meters and overdue badges. Text uses **Wine Ink** (danger-ink) on danger-soft.
- **Status Teal** (success): the "good" tone. Text uses success-ink on success-soft.

### Neutral
- **Mist Base** (mist-base): the page colour under the gradient mist (see grad-page in Gradients).
- **Teal Ink** (teal-ink): all body and heading text. **Muted Ink** (muted-ink): secondary text, descriptions and placeholders. **Sidebar Ink** (sidebar-ink): inactive navigation labels.
- **Glass Fill** (glass-fill, fading to 26% white): the card body. **Popover Glass** (popover-glass, 95% → 86% white): dialogs, popovers, menus and sheets.
- **Glass Edge** (glass-edge): the one-pixel white inner top highlight on glass; outer panel borders are white at 70-85%. **Glass Tint** (glass-tint): the pale end of the active tab gradient. **Hairline** (hairline): dividers and generic borders.
- **Input Stroke** (input-stroke): form-field borders, kept at ≥3:1 against glass for WCAG 1.4.11.
- **Mint Secondary** (mint-secondary, mint gradient): secondary buttons and the primary-tone badge.
- **Track** (track): the empty channel of sliders, progress bars and score bars. **Control Off** (control-off): an unchecked switch. **Neutral Wash** (neutral-soft): the start of the neutral badge gradient.
- **Scrim** (scrim, at 30%): the veil behind dialogs and sheets.

### Gradients
Every gradient is a token in `:root`; these are its stops.
- **grad-page** (fixed mist): radial blooms #a6ccce (top left), #b1b7dc (top right), #aab6d9 (bottom right), #a9cfcf (bottom left) over a 160deg wash #cae0e0 → #d1dae8.
- **grad-card** / **grad-card-strong** / **grad-sidebar**: white glass at 46% → 26%; 95% → rgb(246 251 250 / 0.86); 40% → rgb(236 246 244 / 0.26) → rgb(232 230 250 / 0.3).
- **grad-primary** #0b6f7a → #0e827f; **grad-primary-hover** #0a6570 → #0c7875 (135deg).
- **grad-hero**: glow rgb(158 232 216 / 0.14) at top right, shade rgb(4 52 60 / 0.45) at bottom left, over 120deg #0a5a67 → #0b6470 → #0b6e74.
- **grad-danger** #c63b58 → #d24d68.
- **Chip gradients** (135deg): teal #0f8f86 → #55c7ba; violet #6f5ce8 → #ad9ffb; pink #e0529a → #ff9ccb; orange #ec7f2e → #ffbf7a; sky #2b7fc7 → #7cc0f2; mint #e3f5f1 → #d3ecf0.
- **Meter gradients** (90deg): good #0b7a80 → #22b3a1; warn #e68a24 → #f7b766; critical #c63b58 → #ef7d92; neutral #7f9d9a → #b5cac8; gap #f3b8c4 → #fbdde3 (accreditation readiness with no evidence yet).
- **Soft tone fills** (135deg): success-soft #e2f6f2 → #d3eee9; info-soft #f0edff → #e4e0fd; danger-soft #fdecef → #fbdfe5; warm-soft #fef3e4 → #fde5cf (warning and gold badges).

### Data
- **Categorical** chart-1 teal, chart-2 violet, chart-3 amber, chart-4 sky, chart-5 pink, used in that order. The set passed the dataviz palette validator (lightness, chroma, colour-vision deficiency, normal vision, contrast); do not reorder or substitute it.
- **Sequential** seq-1 → seq-6: one-hue teal ramp for heatmaps and magnitude.
- **Chart details:** the trend line gradient starts at teal-deep; chart dots carry a white dot-ring.
- **Diverging** div-neg-2 → div-mid → div-pos-2: coral to teal with a grey-teal midpoint, for Likert answer distributions.

### Named Rules
**The One Teal Voice Rule.** Deep teal gradient marks action and "you are here": primary buttons, the active navigation pill, banners. Never use it as a card or section fill.

**The Ink Pair Rule.** Every tinted tone has a fill token and a separate darker ink token. Text on a tint always uses the ink (success-ink, warm-orange-ink, violet-ink, danger-ink), never the fill hue itself.

**The Apricot-Only-on-Teal Rule.** On the banner teal, text is white or white at 85%, and the single highlight colour is Banner Apricot.

**The Token-Only Color Rule.** Components never carry hex or rgb literals. Every colour, gradient and shadow is a `:root` token exposed through a theme colour or utility; a new colour needs a named token first.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Plus Jakarta Sans (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Label/Mono Font:** ui-monospace for record codes only (finding IDs such as TMN-2026-0003, indicator codes)

**Character:** One friendly geometric sans in five weights (400-800) with the ss01 and cv11 stylistic sets on. Headings and numbers go extrabold and slightly tight; everything else stays quiet at regular to semibold.

### Hierarchy
- **Banner headline** (800, 36px at every width, line-height 1.12, tight tracking): the login brand-panel headline only. It is the one size above Display and lives only on the banner teal.
- **Display** (800, 30px, tight tracking): the greeting on the dashboard banner and the portal banner (24px on mobile).
- **Headline** (800, 28px desktop / 24px mobile, tight tracking): page titles in the page header, under the breadcrumb.
- **Stat** (800, 28px, line-height 1, tabular): KPI tile values. Smaller KPI figures step down to 24px or 20px at the same weight.
- **Title** (600-800, 16px): card titles, the SIMUTU wordmark, empty-state titles, questionnaire titles and lead paragraphs; dialog titles step up to 18px.
- **Body** (400-500, 14px): descriptions, table cells, sidebar navigation items, indicator statements, form text (inputs use 16px on mobile to prevent zoom). Descriptions cap at max-w-3xl.
- **Label** (600, 13px): KPI tile labels, score-bar labels, form labels.
- **Caption** (500, 12px) and 11px: table headers (12px), hints, badge text, calendar cells (12px), chart axes, meta rows. Nothing in the app goes below 11px.
- **Nav group** (600, 11px, 0.12em tracking, uppercase): sidebar group headings only (UTAMA, PENJAMINAN MUTU...).

### Named Rules
**The Tabular Numbers Rule.** Scores, counts, percentages and dates in data displays use tabular figures so columns and meters align.

**The Uppercase Is Navigation Rule.** Tracked uppercase belongs only to sidebar group headings. Page and card headings stay in sentence case.

## Layout

A left sidebar with a fluid content column. The sidebar is 272px wide on desktop and collapses to a 76px icon rail (280px inside the mobile sheet); below `lg` it moves into a left sheet opened from the topbar. A sticky 64px glass topbar holds search (Ctrl K), the active-period pill, notifications and the user menu. Content sits inside a 1400px max-width container with 16px / 24px / 32px side padding (mobile / md / lg) and 24px / 32px vertical padding.

Rhythm is a 4px base: 24px between page sections and card grids, 24px card padding, 20px KPI tile padding, 12-16px inside list rows, 8px between inline controls. Dashboards use 4-up KPI rows over 2-up (wide + narrow) chart rows that collapse to a single column on mobile. Students on the portal get a single column with the banner on top and full-width cards.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth comes from translucency plus soft shadows. Glass panels blur and saturate what sits behind them (22-28px blur, 1.35-1.4 saturation), take a white inner top highlight, and cast a long, soft, teal-tinted shadow offset downwards. Floating layers (dialogs, popovers, menus, tooltips) are more opaque and cast a deeper shadow. Coloured elements (primary button, active pill, icon chips) cast a shadow in their own hue.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Card** (`inset 0 1px 0 rgb(255 255 255 / 0.75), 0 18px 40px -22px rgb(20 70 76 / 0.35), 0 2px 8px -3px rgb(20 70 76 / 0.1)`): every resting glass card and tile.
- **Float** (`0 24px 60px -24px rgb(20 60 76 / 0.45), 0 4px 14px -6px rgb(20 60 76 / 0.15)`): dialogs, popovers, dropdowns, tooltips.
- **Primary glow** (`0 10px 22px -10px rgb(11 111 122 / 0.65)`): the primary button and active navigation pill.
- **Danger glow** (`0 10px 22px -10px rgb(198 59 88 / 0.6)`): destructive button.
- **Soft** (`0 6px 16px -10px rgb(20 70 76 / 0.35)`): outline buttons and small glass controls.
- **Chip glows** (`0 8px 18px -8px` in the chip hue: teal rgb(15 143 134 / 0.6), violet rgb(111 92 232 / 0.6), pink rgb(224 82 154 / 0.6), orange rgb(236 127 46 / 0.6), sky rgb(43 127 199 / 0.55)): gradient icon chips.
- **Seal drop** (`drop-shadow(0 12px 24px rgb(0 0 0 / 0.35))`): the institution seal on the teal login panel.

### Named Rules
**The Two Glass Weights Rule.** Resting content uses thin glass (46% → 26% white, card shadow). Anything that floats above content uses strong glass (95% → 86% white, float shadow). There is no third opacity.

## Shapes

Soft, generous rounding. The base radius is 14px. Cards, tiles, dialogs and list rows inside cards use 22px; banners use 24px; every button size (xs through lg, including icon buttons), the active navigation pill and icon chips use 18px; inputs use 12px; dialog and sheet close buttons use 14px; keyboard focus outlines follow a 10px radius; status badges, meters and counters are full pills. Every glass panel carries a one-pixel white border (70-85% opacity) in place of a grey outline.

**The One Button Radius Rule.** All buttons share the 18px radius regardless of size; sizes change height and padding, never the corner.

## Components

### Buttons
Gradient, soft-shadowed and confident.
- **Shape:** gently rounded (18px) at every size; default 36px tall (xs 24px, sm 32px, lg 40px), semibold 14px.
- **Primary:** action-teal gradient, white text, primary glow shadow. Hover brightens 10%, active dims 5%.
- **Outline:** white glass gradient (85% → 45%) with a white border, backdrop blur and the soft shadow; brightens to solid white on hover. Used for secondary actions on glass and on the banner.
- **Secondary:** mint gradient with mint-secondary ink.
- **Destructive:** coral gradient with the danger glow.
- **Ghost:** transparent, 55% white wash on hover. **Link:** action-teal text, underlined on hover.
- **Focus:** 3px action-teal ring at 80%. On the banner teal, focus rings turn white.

### Chips (status badges)
- **Style:** full pill, 12px semibold, a leading 6px dot in the text colour, a soft gradient from the tone's wash to 70% white, and a one-pixel inset ring in the tone.
- **Tones:** success, warning (orange ink), danger, info (violet), primary (mint), neutral. Status maps to tone in one table: overdue/open/rejected are danger, pending/reopened are warning, in progress/review are info, verified/active are success.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 22px.
- **Background:** thin glass (46% → 26% white at 150deg) with 22px blur.
- **Shadow Strategy:** Card shadow (see Elevation & Depth).
- **Border:** 1px white at 75%.
- **Internal Padding:** 24px; KPI tiles 20px.

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** 36px tall, 12px radius, white gradient fill (85% → 55%), input-stroke border.
- **Focus:** border turns action-teal with a 3px action-teal ring at 80%.
- **Error / Disabled:** error switches the border to danger with a faint danger ring; disabled drops to 50% opacity.

### Navigation
- **Sidebar:** light glass (40% white fading to a lavender tint), white right edge, heavy blur. Items are 40px tall, 14px medium in sidebar ink with action-teal icons at 75%.
- **Active:** full teal gradient pill with white text and icon and a primary glow. Active children in a group show a teal dot on a thin teal guide line.
- **Hover:** 60% white wash.
- **Counters:** orange gradient pills with on-orange (Umber) text.
- **Mobile:** the same sidebar in a left sheet; the topbar shows a menu button.

### Hero Banner (signature)
The one dark surface in the world: banner teal with a faint dotted sparkle pattern and a blurred teal bloom, 24px radius, white display greeting, 85% white meta line with the active period in Banner Apricot, and outline or white buttons on the right. It appears once per screen at most (dashboard, portal home, login brand panel, lecturer analytics summary).

### KPI Tile and Icon Chip (signature)
A glass tile holding a 13px label, a 28px extrabold value and a caption hint, with an optional 36px gradient icon chip (teal, orange, violet, pink or sky) at top right casting a glow in its own hue.

### Meters and Score Bars
Full-pill tracks tinted in the tone (teal 12%, warning 18%, coral 12%) with gradient fills: good is teal, warning is orange, critical is coral, neutral is grey-teal, and the readiness gap (no evidence yet) is pale rose. Score bars, sliders and progress bars sit on the Track colour; score bars show a threshold tick and colour below-threshold bars coral.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** put every content surface on thin glass (46% → 26% white, 22px blur, 1px white edge, card shadow) over the fixed mist background.
- **Do** use the action-teal gradient only for primary actions, the active navigation pill and "good" meters; use action-teal (#07505a) for teal text and icons.
- **Do** pair every tinted fill with its ink token for text (success-ink, warm-orange-ink, violet-ink, danger-ink) to hold 4.5:1.
- **Do** use the validated categorical order chart-1 → chart-5, the teal seq ramp for magnitude, and the coral ↔ teal diverging ramp for Likert distributions.
- **Do** keep input borders at input-stroke so fields stay ≥3:1 on glass.
- **Do** add a named `:root` token before using any new colour, gradient or shadow in a component.
- **Do** switch focus rings to white on the banner teal.
- **Do** honour reduced motion: keep fades, drop scale, slide and width animations.

### Don't:
- **Don't** return to the flat admin look: grey page, flat white cards, dark sidebar.
- **Don't** use pure black text; ink is teal-ink.
- **Don't** fill cards or sections with accent hues (violet, pink, orange, sky). They belong to icon chips, badges, counters and charts.
- **Don't** lighten the banner back to the first-sketch teal (#0f7f86 → #16a39a); white text on it would fail AA.
- **Don't** vary button corner radius by size.
- **Don't** add a dark mode; the product is light-only by decision.
- **Don't** carry this world into the PDF reports (resources/views/reports); they stay plain for print.
